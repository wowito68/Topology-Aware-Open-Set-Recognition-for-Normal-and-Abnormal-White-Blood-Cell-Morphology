1. Evaluating Open-Set Reliability in Leukocyte Morphology Classification under Domain Shift

Rationale: this title accurately frames the study as an evaluation of reliability, not as a new architecture, diagnosis system, ArcFace method, or topology method. It names the biomedical task and the external-validity stressor without overstating novelty.
